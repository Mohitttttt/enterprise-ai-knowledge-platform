COMPANY EMPLOYEE POLICY

1. Annual Leave

Employees receive 30 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Annual leave requests must be submitted to the employee's manager for approval.

2. Sick Leave

Employees must notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible when they are unable to attend work due to illness.

3. Password Recovery

Employees can reset their company passwords through the employee portal.
If they cannot access the portal, they should contact the IT Support team.
Employees must never share their passwords with another person.

4. Working Hours

Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
Any changes to an employee's working schedule must be agreed with their manager.
