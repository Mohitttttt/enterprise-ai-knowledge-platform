IT SUPPORT POLICY
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 January 2026

1. Account Lockouts
Employees whose accounts become locked after multiple unsuccessful login attempts must contact the IT Support team. Employees must provide their company email address and a description of the issue. IT Support will verify the employee's identity before restoring account access.

2. Software Installation
Employees must submit a software installation request to IT Support. The request must include the software name, business purpose, and device information. Employees must not install unapproved software on company devices.

3. Hardware Problems
Employees experiencing problems with company laptops, keyboards, monitors, or other equipment should submit a support request. IT Support will assess the issue and arrange repair or replacement when necessary.

4. Support Response
IT Support aims to acknowledge standard support requests within one business day. Critical incidents affecting multiple employees are prioritised.
