COMPANY EMPLOYEE POLICY Version: 1.0 Effective date: 1 January 2026

1.  Annual Leave Employees receive 30 days of paid annual leave per
    calendar year. Annual leave requests must be submitted to the
    employee’s manager for approval.

2.  Sick Leave Employees who are unable to work due to illness must
    notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible. Employees must
    follow the company’s applicable absence-reporting procedures.

3.  Password Recovery Employees can reset their company passwords
    through the employee portal. If they cannot access the portal, they
    should contact the IT Support team. Employees must never share their
    passwords with another person.

4.  Working Hours Standard working hours are Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM
    to 5:00 PM. Any changes to an employee’s working schedule must be
    agreed with their manager.

5.  Remote Work Remote work arrangements must be approved by the
    employee’s manager. Employees working remotely must follow company
    information-security policies.

6.  IT Support Employees can contact the IT Support team for help with
    company devices, software access, and account issues. Support
    requests should include a clear description of the problem.

7.  Maternity and Parental Leave This policy document does not specify
    maternity or parental leave entitlements. Employees should contact
    Human Resources for information about applicable leave policies.
