REMOTE WORK POLICY
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 January 2026

1. Eligibility
Employees may request remote work arrangements when their job responsibilities can be performed outside the office. Remote work is not an automatic employee entitlement.

2. Approval
All remote work arrangements must receive approval from the employee's manager before they begin. Managers may consider business requirements, team coordination, and employee responsibilities when reviewing requests.

3. Equipment
Employees working remotely may request company-approved equipment through IT Support. Company equipment must be used in accordance with IT and information-security policies.

4. Working Hours
Employees working remotely must remain available during their agreed working schedule. Any changes to working hours must be agreed with their manager.

5. Information Security
Employees must protect confidential company information when working remotely. Public or unsecured networks should not be used to access sensitive company systems without approved security protection.
