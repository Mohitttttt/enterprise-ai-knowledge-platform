EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 January 2026

1. Eligible Expenses
Employees may request reimbursement for reasonable business expenses incurred while performing approved work activities.

2. Prior Approval
Business travel and accommodation expenses must receive manager approval before the expense is incurred.

3. Required Documentation
Employees must submit an expense claim with original receipts or valid digital receipts. Claims must include the expense date, amount, business purpose, and relevant project or department.

4. Submission Deadline
Expense claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense date.

5. Reimbursement Processing
Approved expense claims are normally processed within 15 business days after complete documentation has been received.

6. Personal Expenses
Personal purchases unrelated to approved business activities are not eligible for reimbursement.
