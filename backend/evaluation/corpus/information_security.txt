INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 January 2026

1. Multi-Factor Authentication
Employees must enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all supported company accounts. Authentication codes must never be shared with another person.

2. Password Security
Employees must use strong, unique passwords for company accounts. Passwords must never be shared with colleagues, managers, or external parties.

3. Phishing Emails
Employees who receive suspicious emails must not open unexpected attachments or follow suspicious links. Suspected phishing emails must be reported to the IT Security team.

4. Data Handling
Confidential company information must only be stored in company-approved systems. Employees must not transfer confidential information to personal cloud storage accounts.

5. Lost Devices
Lost or stolen company devices must be reported to IT Support immediately. Employees should provide the device type and the approximate time and location of the incident.

6. Security Incidents
Employees must report suspected unauthorised access or disclosure of company information to the IT Security team immediately.
