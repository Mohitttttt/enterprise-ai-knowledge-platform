EMPLOYEE BENEFITS POLICY
Version: 1.0
Effective date: 1 January 2026

1. Professional Development
Eligible employees may request reimbursement for approved professional training courses. The request must be approved by the employee's manager before the course is purchased.

2. Training Budget
The annual professional development budget is EUR 500 per eligible employee. Unused training budgets cannot be transferred to the following calendar year.

3. Transport Allowance
Eligible employees receive a monthly public transport allowance of EUR 49. Employees must submit valid proof of purchase to Human Resources.

4. Health Insurance
Employees must contact Human Resources for information about company health insurance benefits and eligibility.

5. Benefit Enquiries
Questions about employee benefits should be directed to the Human Resources department.
